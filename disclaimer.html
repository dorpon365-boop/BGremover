Check the official rules

Each authority sets its own requirements for photo size, head size and position, background color, expression, glasses, head coverings, lighting, print quality, and file type. These rules can change. Always read the current rules from the official website of the authority you are applying to before you submit or print your photo.

What the tool does and does not do

  • It removes the background and crops to common sizes: 35×45 mm, 2×2 inch (51×51 mm), 20×25 mm stamp size, 25×35 mm, and square.
  • It does not check the face, expression, head size, shadows, or whether the photo meets any specific authority's rules.
  • AI background removal can be imperfect, especially around hair and clothing. Zoom in and check the edges before you use the photo.
  • For printing, print at 100% scale (no "fit to page") and use the print sheet or PDF at 300 DPI or higher. Printers and software can change the final size.
